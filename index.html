About

I am an engineering student at Kyambogo University pursuing a Bachelor of Engineering in Mechatronics and Biomedical Engineering, an independent researcher, and a science communicator.

My focus is neural engineering. My long-term goal is to design and build engineering systems that enable people with neurological conditions to live more functional, independent lives.

Alongside engineering, I produce long-form technical breakdowns on YouTube, translating complex neurotechnology and biomedical engineering concepts into accessible, research-backed videos.
